## 2024-07-01 - [Interactive Accordion Accessibility]
**Learning:** [Interactive accordion components like FAQs require explicit ARIA relationships (aria-expanded, aria-controls, role="region", aria-labelledby) and focus-visible styling to ensure proper screen reader support and keyboard accessibility.]
**Action:** [When building or modifying interactive accordions, always include type="button", complete ARIA state attributes mapping the toggle button to the content container, and explicit focus-visible styles.]
